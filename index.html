The Property

A Different Kind of Mountain Escape

Set against the green hills of Poombarai, Luxury Farm House offers a distinctive hillside setting with elevated cabins and beautiful natural surroundings. Each cabin is individually positioned along the mountain slope, offering privacy and uninterrupted views of the valley below.

Architecture & Property

Designed for the Hillside

Unlike conventional hill resort structures, our cabins are engineered on secure stilt foundations to harmonize with the steep mountain contours of Poombarai.

Elevated Stilt Design

Individual structures raised on secure steel pillars along the hillside slope, preserving the natural ground vegetation while floating gently over the landscape.

Private Balconies

Each cabin opens onto an expansive private viewing deck that looks directly out into the valleys of Poombarai, offering front-row seats to drifting morning clouds.

Staggered Privacy

Strategically spaced at varying elevations along the slope to ensure each cabin enjoys complete visual seclusion and silent mountain tranquility.
