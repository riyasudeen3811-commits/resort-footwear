The Property

Luxury Farm House

Luxury Farm House is a hillside property situated in Poombarai, a serene village in the Kodaikanal region of Tamil Nadu's Western Ghats. The property features individual elevated cabins — each one distinctively positioned along the natural slope of the mountain.

Unlike conventional hotels, the architecture works with the terrain rather than against it. Cabins are raised on supporting structures, providing elevation that frames the surrounding valley and mountain views from every angle.

Individual Hillside Cabins Western Ghats Valley Views

The Architecture

Built with the Hillside

The elevated cabin design is what makes Luxury Farm House visually and experientially distinctive. Each cabin sits above the natural contour of the hill, supported by visible structural foundations, with large windows and open balconies that bring the outside in.

The placement of each cabin at a different height along the slope ensures privacy and provides every guest with their own unobstructed perspective of the mountain landscape.

The Location

Poombarai, Tamil Nadu

Poombarai is a quiet hill village in the Kodaikanal region, known for its terraced agricultural slopes, ancient Murugan temple and unspoilt natural surroundings. The village sits at a significant elevation in the Western Ghats, surrounded by rolling green hills and deep valleys.

Luxury Farm House is located at 79CW+XG9, Yaanai Vattan, Poombarai, Tamil Nadu 624103 — accessible from Kodaikanal town and positioned to take full advantage of the region's dramatic mountain topography.
